# Publisher reader, Home and Shorts

The news product uses direct AT Protocol posts, independently of RSS, outlet APIs, News V2 and editorial curation. The editable Frontpage V2 remains separate. Social Home, profiles and menus retain their existing behavior.

## Sources and feeds

`publishers.ts` holds fifteen operator-selected accounts with stable IDs, DIDs and domains: WIRED, The Verge, Financial Times, Euractiv, 404 Media, Reuters, The Associated Press, The Guardian, CNN, NPR, FRANCE 24, POLITICO Europe, DER SPIEGEL, DIE ZEIT and Le Monde. The ten added accounts were checked against public Bluesky profiles; directory names, avatars, bios and counts still come from real profiles. `findPublisherByDid()` identifies source posts and profile redirects.

`PublisherFeedAPI` reads author feeds in batches of six concurrent requests. It interleaves source queues round-robin, preserving each source's order and the current turn across pages. Only original posts from the last seven days qualify; reposts, replies and duplicate post URIs are excluded. Shorts requests `posts_with_video` and excludes GIFs. Per-source queues/cursors are kept in the existing post-feed query API instance; refresh resets them. Failed sources do not hide successful sources, remain retryable, and total failure reaches normal query error UI. Cancellation is forwarded to XRPC.

## Home

`src/screens/NewsHome/` replaces news Home/Start, including public signed-out web Home, without mounting either social Home implementation or its welcome prompt. Signed-in readers get full-width Following and Discover tabs; signed-out readers get public Discover without graph requests. Following selects registered publishers actually followed by the current account, including optimistic follow/unfollow shadows, and offers three genuine Follow suggestions when empty. Article descriptors isolate registry-ordered source subsets from Discover. Shared moderation, original post identity and stable append-only `ArticleList` bands remain. Home and Publisher automatically load near the end, retaining loading/empty/error/retry UI. Web observes a bottom sentinel rather than treating the document's full content height as the viewport; native uses bounded scroll measurements.

Home uses the same inner padding and wide geometry as publisher details. Compact typography/media bounds prevent full-width fallbacks from becoming oversized heroes. Source badges show the real moderated avatar and source name and link to `/publisher/:publisher`. Small/compact articles put the badge above date/actions; larger articles keep them together. Dim uses a lighter badge surface. Wide small companions and equal pairs show up to four lines of genuine description. Large preview videos use side-by-side video/text on wide screens, with height capped at 80% of the window; narrow screens stack normally. Article likes use shared authenticated like/unlike queues and shadows; comments open the original thread.

After the first four visible articles, Home inserts a horizontal Shorts row of real video thumbnails, post captions and publisher names. It shares the Shorts query/cache, mounts no video players, respects moderation/deletion and omits unavailable view counts. Following filters thumbnails to followed publishers but keeps the same global Shorts cache, so every selected clip exists in the destination playlist. Cards open `/shorts?initialPostUri=…` at the selected clip; See all opens the ordinary Shorts feed. The two article sections retain their collection offset and append-only bands.

## Directory and details

- `PublisherDirectory` at `/publishers` (`/publisher` alias) has two-column wide-web cards and one-column mobile/native cards. Real moderated avatars, names/handles, genuine badges, bios, follower counts and shared Follow controls; no fabricated statistics. Directory queries profiles only, never all author feeds.
- `Publisher` at `/publisher/:publisher` has a larger scrolling identity/Follow block, real follower/post counts (no following count), rich bio and safe profile links. Its ordinary back button scrolls away. Direct-entry back falls back to the directory; invalid IDs show not-found/Browse publishers.
- Details use chronological moderated author-feed originals and automatic pagination. Shared newspaper bands pair each feature with only one smaller story. Reversed features put that companion first on the left, preserving visual and DOM reading order. Appending never regroups existing bands.
- Normal Follow/Follow back/Following copy, permissions, mutation queues and profile shadows are reused. There are no subscription relationships, editing/curation controls or layout records.

## Profiles and threads

News profiles for configured publishers replace their route with the Publisher detail page after DID resolution (direct DIDs redirect immediately). Other news profiles have no tab controls and use `publisher-replies|DID`: only the profile author's replies whose **root** post belongs to a configured publisher, including nested replies. A publisher immediate parent with a nonpublisher root does not qualify. Existing header, profile moderation and authentication remain shared.

News publisher originals use shared article bodies in selected and ancestor thread positions, and above comments in the profile replies feed. The latter retains feed identity, reply connectors, content-list moderation, translations, original actions and post shadows instead of rendering a duplicate social external card. Replies remain ordinary comments. The entire news thread, including replies, uses wide desktop geometry. Inline photos/videos, including quotes and replies, cap at 80% of viewport height while preserving aspect ratios; fullscreen viewers and immersive Shorts remain unrestricted. Ancestors retain their list filtering boundary; selected posts retain deletion/reporting/content moderation, translation, reply/threadgate and action safeguards. Equivalent captions/headlines/summaries are suppressed. Dates/actions remain with more spacing; expanded engagement/known-liker rows are omitted. Social and reader-mode threads are unchanged.

## Shorts

`/shorts` is a public web destination in news menus, using `publishers|videos`. Web uses full-height vertical scroll snapping, keyboard/desktop previous-next controls and reduced-motion-aware scrolling. Only the selected focused clip mounts a shared video player; other clips are thumbnails. Shared content hiding, post shadows, avatar moderation, Follow, likes, conversations, sharing and reporting remain in place. `Shorts/Actions.tsx` scopes the shared post controls to a high-contrast overlay presentation: transparent targets, white 30px icons, white counts below, consistent hit areas and subtle shadows. Liked hearts retain the normal accent. Default feed/thread controls and the underlying auth/mutation/menu behavior are unchanged. Native reuses the existing immersive VideoFeed and pooled players, with a publisher source context.

The web Shorts screen and desktop sidebar use a scoped dark ALF context, leaving saved theme, accent and typography preferences untouched. Captions use smaller regular text, with More/Less only when clipped. The full-bottom fade is rendered below playback controls, avoiding both the old hard edge and dimmed controls. Captions, action rail, fade, navigation and player controls hide together after two seconds; interaction wakes them without pausing playback, while open menus and keyboard-focused controls remain usable. Explicit mute/unmute choices carry across clips. There is no top-left close button; Back/Escape remain.

News signed-in menus are Home, Publisher, Shorts, Saved and Profile; public menus are Home, Publisher and Shorts. Settings remains directly available but omitted from menus. Native retains the existing authentication shell; physical-device playback, history, auth and account-switch checks remain pending. When introducing new `.web.tsx` variants into a running Expo process, restart Metro with `--clear` if its cached resolver still chooses the native entry point.
